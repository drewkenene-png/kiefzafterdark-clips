# Clip Pipeline

#clips #project

How a clip gets from the phone to a post.

## Steps
1. **Record** on phone.
2. **Move to PC** – USB cable is fastest. Plug in, choose *File Transfer*, open `DCIM`, drag to the PC. Dropbox works too for single clips.
3. **Sort** into `Clips/Raw/<date>` on the PC.
4. **Edit** – trim, caption, add music.
5. **Post** – see [[Clips/Posting Checklist]].
6. **Log it** in [[Clips/Clip Log]].

## Folders on PC
```
Clips/
  Raw/        untouched phone footage
  Edited/     finished exports
  Thumbs/     thumbnails
```
