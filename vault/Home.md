## How this vault works
- **Folders** on the left group notes by project.
- **[[Double brackets]]** link notes together. Type `[[` and start typing a note name.
- **Tags** like #todo and #idea show up in the tag pane on the right.
- **Templates** live in `Templates/`. Press `Ctrl+T` inside any note to insert one.
- **Daily notes** go in `Daily/`. Press `Ctrl+D` to open today's.
